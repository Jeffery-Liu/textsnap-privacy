TextSnap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-14

TextSnap ("the app") is a screenshot text-recognition (OCR) tool that runs entirely on your own device. Your privacy is simple to describe because the app is built to keep everything local.

What we collect

Nothing. TextSnap does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking.

Your screenshots and text

Screenshots you capture and the text recognized from them are processed locally on your device using an on-device OCR model. They are never uploaded, sent to any server, or shared with anyone. The app does not require an internet connection to function.

Clipboard

When you choose to copy text or an image, it is placed on your system clipboard — this is standard operating-system behavior and stays on your device.

Local settings

Your preferences (such as the chosen hotkey and text layout) are stored in small files on your own device and are never transmitted.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.

TextSnap — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-08-14

TextSnap（"本应用"）是一款完全在你本地设备上运行的截图文字识别（OCR）工具。它的隐私政策很简单，因为应用从设计上就把一切都留在本地。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。 TextSnap 不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人数据。没有账号、无需登录，也没有任何分析或追踪。

你的截图与文字

你截取的图片以及识别出的文字，都在本地设备上用设备内置的 OCR 模型处理，绝不上传、不发送到任何服务器、不与任何人共享。应用运行也不需要联网。

剪贴板

当你选择复制文字或图片时，内容会被放入系统剪贴板——这是操作系统的标准行为，内容始终留在你的设备上。

本地设置

你的偏好设置（如所选快捷键、文本排版方式）保存在你设备上的小文件里，绝不外传。

儿童隐私

本应用是通用工具，不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的任何信息。

政策变更

若本政策有变更，更新后的版本会发布在同一网址，并标注新的"最后更新"日期。
